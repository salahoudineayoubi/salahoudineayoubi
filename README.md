### About

I'm the technical co-founder of **KolayExpress**, a cross-border commerce platform connecting suppliers in **Türkiye and the Middle East** with buyers across **Africa**. I designed and ship the whole stack: marketplace, supplier and forwarder tools, payments, logistics and AI features, on web, mobile and backend.

I'm also **General Coordinator of the Lap Nomba Foundation**, a Cameroonian non-profit that steers young people away from online fraud ("Zaguina") through awareness, digital training and professional integration.

I care about software that holds up in production: typed code, automated tests, secure defaults and fast iteration. I work daily with **AI coding agents** and the engineering discipline needed to run them safely.

Based in **Yaoundé, Cameroon** · working with teams in Istanbul · open to remote.
